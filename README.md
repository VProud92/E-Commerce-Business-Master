# 🚀 E-Commerce Business Master: Free Google Sheets Template

A clean, automated, and professional Google Sheets template designed specifically for small e-commerce businesses (Garments, Aroma Candles, Mobile Accessories, and more) to track sales, expenses, inventory, and monthly profit without any expensive software.

---

## ✨ Key Features (اہم خصوصیات)
* **📊 Executive Dashboard:** Real-time summary of Total Sales, Total Expenses, and Net Profit.
* **📅 Date Range Filter & Calendar Picker:** Easily filter your financial data by custom start and end dates with built-in Google Sheets calendar dropdowns.
* **📦 Product Inventory Tracker:** Automated SKU generation (`PRD-001`, `PRD-002`) and multi-category management (Garments, Candles, Accessories).
* **🛒 Daily Orders & Sales Tracker:** Systematic record keeping for customer details, shipping addresses, quantities, and payment methods.
* **💸 Expense Manager:** Keep track of business expenses (packaging, courier, marketing, utilities) to get accurate net profit calculations.
* **🔌 Open Source & Plug-and-Play:** 100% free and ready to use. Easily linkable with websites, Google Forms, or WhatsApp ordering systems in the future!

---

## 🛠️ How to Use (استعمال کرنے کا طریقہ)
1. Click on the template link below to open it in Google Sheets.
2. Go to **File > Make a copy** to save your own editable version in your Google Drive.
3. Start adding your products in the **Inventory** tab and record your daily orders in the **Orders & Sales** tab.
4. Watch your **Dashboard** automatically calculate your business performance!
